## Preserved failures

On prior head `f38b73b03a`, [context job 121932226397](https://github.com/c933103/openrailwaystyle/actions/runs/40425501815/job/121932226397) timed out at the unchanged 45-second signed-height predicate. Its new diagnostics finally distinguish the conjuncts: source `terrain3d`, pitch 60°, sea **0 m**, land **340 m**, style/source loaded; fine/coarse/water request counts 7/16/7; 16 encoded worker replies and no worker/map/page errors. The original state JSON, screenshot and timings are archived. The uniform beige synthetic screenshot alone is not visual proof of signed terrain.

An actual `vt-pbf` → worker `VectorTile`/`Pbf` round trip proves the fixture supplied the wrong geometry representation: `{x,y}` objects become a zero-area ring at `(0,0)`. The page's stub decoder hid that corruption. The correction supplies a closed `[x,y]` rectangle, imports the real built page decoder and tests identical geometry in both production-page and worker decoders. The explicit closing point is required because `vt-pbf` omits it when encoding polygon commands. `water-mvt-reproduction.json` preserves exact synthetic old/new bytes, hashes and decoded geometry.

Separately, [infrastructure job 121932226440](https://github.com/c933103/openrailwaystyle/actions/runs/40425501815/job/121932226440) timed out on mobile at the original 30-second zoom-18 platform predicate. No post-failure state existed; the archived pre-failure screenshot only shows `1 · 810 ft` at zoom 19. The cause remains **unproven**. The added diagnostics preserve each conjunct, query errors, submitted/source/rendered geometry, source/map state, bounded event history and a screenshot before rethrowing the original error. They do not retry or extend acceptance. A CPU controller/geometry check passes desktop/mobile 19→18 with visible-edge input; it is not browser-event or tile-order proof.

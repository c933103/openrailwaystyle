Terrain and seabed data

Relief uses Mapzen Terrain Tiles hosted by AWS. Elevation sources vary by location and resolution. Ocean-floor relief comes from NOAA ETOPO1; this is not a navigational chart.

Near the poles, beyond 85.05° N and S where these tiles end, the globe's relief and contours come from NOAA ETOPO 2022 (60 arc-second surface elevation; NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, U.S. Government material not subject to copyright protection within the United States), prepared in advance for the two polar caps.

Contours are calculated from the same elevation tiles with maplibre-contour (BSD-3-Clause license). Brown contours show land elevation and blue contours show negative elevation below sea level, in metres. Spacing adapts to zoom; seabed resolution remains limited by the source data.

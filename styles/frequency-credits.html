Service frequency sources

Coverage is partial. Frequency widths use current timetable counts matched to OpenStreetMap service sections and audited whole-route headways where available. These are dated schedules or reference estimates, not live departures or a guarantee of service. Sections without a current matched frequency profile are subdued; an independently available departure board does not establish frequency coverage. A measured zero remains an available profile and is drawn as a thin, subdued line; unknown is not zero.

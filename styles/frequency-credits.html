Railway Atlas

Service frequency sources

Coverage is partial. The same AM peak, PM peak and off-peak width scale is used worldwide. These are dated weekday schedule/reference profiles, not live departures or a guarantee of service. Unknown and expired profiles are subdued; zero is retained separately. Where directions differ, width uses the lower available directional rate. Listed GTFS windows are configured in each agency's local timezone, not claimed operator peak definitions. Profiles expire at the earlier of feed validity and the 30-day source review interval.
